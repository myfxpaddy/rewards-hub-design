The five ways we check

On-chain, every day

Hold, lock, trade, buy, packages · daily snapshot
  1. 1MemberBuys and holds CMX-R, locks it in the Vault, trades on CrymadX or buys a CMX HUB package. Nothing to submit.
  2. 2CrymadXTakes a snapshot of balances and Vault locks once a day and totals trades from our own records.
  3. 3CrymadXAdds On-chain XP: per day held or locked, per dollar traded, once per package.
Fully automatic and the hardest to fake. Trades between a member's own accounts are removed before XP is counted.

Joined the server or group

Discord, Telegram, YouTube, CMX HUB · seconds
  1. 1MemberVerifies Discord and Telegram once with their login buttons, and YouTube with Google sign-in.
  2. 2MemberJoins the CMX Official Discord, the Telegram group or the YouTube channel, then taps Verify.
  3. 3CrymadXOur Discord and Telegram bots confirm the member is inside; YouTube confirms the subscription.
Re-checked before payout, so leaving removes the XP.

Message link

Weekly Discord and Telegram conversations · seconds
  1. 1MemberStarts or joins a conversation, then pastes the link to their own message.
  2. 2CrymadXOur bot opens the message and checks it was written this week by the verified account and is long enough to be real.
  3. 3CrymadXAdds Community XP, up to 3 times a week and once a day.
Spam, copy-paste and one-word messages are refused. Moderators can strike a message, which removes its XP.

Post link

Weekly like and repost on X · about a minute
  1. 1MemberLikes and reposts or quotes one of our latest 3 posts, adding their personal code when quoting.
  2. 2MemberPastes the link.
  3. 3CrymadXChecks the public post belongs to the verified X account and refers to one of our latest 3 posts.
Avoids paying X for follower lookups. Follows on X are spot-checked instead.

Quiz

Learn quests · instant
  1. 1MemberWatches a short video about CMX-R, wrapped CMX-R or CMX HUB packages.
  2. 2MemberAnswers 3 questions.
  3. 3CrymadXAdds Community XP when all 3 are right. A wrong answer can be retried the next day.
Teaches the ecosystem before people buy, which means fewer support tickets later.

Fair play

  • Identity verification on CrymadX is required before any XP is paid out in CMX-R.
  • One Discord, Telegram and X account per CrymadX account.
  • Leaving the server or unfollowing removes that quest's XP.
  • Bots, bought followers or copied content void the season's XP.
  • No referral rewards in this season, as requested.
